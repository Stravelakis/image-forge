## What is it?

Image Forge is a picture-making machine on your computer.

You tell it what you want — *"twelve cute potion bottles"* — and it asks a
robot artist on the internet to draw them. Then it puts all the drawings in a
folder with sensible names.

## Does it cost money?

Not if you don't want it to. Some robot artists are free. One of them doesn't
even need you to sign up.

Some robot artists cost a little money for each picture. Image Forge will
**always ask you first** before using one of those, and tell you how much.

## Is it safe?

To use some robot artists you need a secret password called a **key**. Image
Forge keeps your keys on your own computer, locked in a box only you can
open. It never sends them anywhere except to the robot artist you picked. If you
also have the video app, the two share keys, so you only type each one once.

## How do I use it?

1. Install it, like any other program.
2. Windows might show a blue warning. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Type what you want in the big box.
4. Say how many.
5. Pick a look if you like, and pick a robot artist from the little list.
6. Press the big button.
7. Wait. The pictures pop up one by one.

## Where do my pictures go?

Into a big picture shelf called **Made**. Every picture you ever made sits there
with the day it was made and a little note about which robot artist drew it.

Don't like one? Press **Redo** and the robot artist tries again. Mark the ones you
don't like with a little cross, then press one button to redo all of them.

## Can it make a cartoon friend who talks?

Yes! It is called a **puppet**. You say *"a grey owl with round glasses"* and
Image Forge draws four faces. You pick your favourite. Then it draws that same
face again and again, with the mouth open a little, open wide, closed, and the
eyes shut, until the puppet can talk and blink. You can even give it arms so it
waves.

Then you press **Send to Video Forge** and the puppet is ready to be in a video.

## A little pet

A tiny purple fox runs after your mouse. It sits down when it catches up and
takes a nap if you stop. You can turn it off, or ask for a different pet.

## Little help bubbles

Hold your mouse over any button and a small bubble tells you what it does.

## It has a friend

There is another app called **Video Forge** (it used to be called BYOK Vid
Creator) that makes videos with talking cartoon characters. If you have both,
they share keys, and the video app can ask Image Forge to draw pictures or even
a whole puppet for it. They help each other.

## Something went wrong?

Usually it just needs a minute — free robot artists are a bit slow. If a
picture looks wrong, you can ask for it again. And if you are stuck, the
people who made it would like to hear about it on GitHub.

## New toys

You can open the picture factory in its own window or in your web browser —
you choose. And when there is a newer factory, one button swaps it in for you.
Your pictures stay right where they are.
